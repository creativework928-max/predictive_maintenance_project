Leakage and Temporal-Safety Assessment

Leakage prevention.
  • UID is not treated as a genuine timestamp for AI4I.
  • Failure-mode label columns are not used as predictive sensor features.
  • Rolling and lag features use current and historical observations only.
  • No future observation is used to construct a past feature.
  • Data-driven imputers/scalers/encoders must be fitted on training data only.
  • Feature selection decisions are based on training data rather than the full dataset.
  • Validation and test data are transformed using parameters learned from training data.
